## 🛡️ Who Am I?

I'm **Muhammad Khalid bin Walid** — a passionate **Junior Red Teamer and Penetration Tester** with a growing specialisation in **AI/LLM Security Research**. My work sits at the intersection of traditional offensive security and emerging threats against intelligent systems.

I focus on identifying vulnerabilities before adversaries do — whether that's exploiting misconfigured web APIs, mapping Active Directory attack paths, or researching **prompt injection**, **model jailbreaking**, and **adversarial ML** vectors against large language models.

**What I bring to the table:**
- Methodical offensive mindset with hands-on red team experience
- Active research into LLM guardrail bypasses and AI risk assessment
- OSINT automation and custom vulnerability tooling
- Clear written communication through [technical blog posts](https://medium.com/@muhammadkhalidbinwalid)

> *"Adversarial thinking for a secure future."*
